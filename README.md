

#  Heart of Stone

##  Overview

**Heart of Stone** is an *action-packed thriller* film featuring a secret agent on a mission to protect a powerful AI weapon known as *“The Heart.”* The movie combines **intense fight scenes**, *clever plot twists*, and a strong female lead.

---

##  Main Characters

1. Rachel Stone – *The skilled and fearless protagonist* 
2. Parker – *Her trusted partner who has secrets of his own* 
3. Keya Dhawan – *A young hacker with a complicated past* 

---

##  Key Themes

* **Trust** and betrayal 
* The *balance between technology and humanity* 
* *Courage* in the face of danger 

---

> “You can’t trust anyone with The Heart — not even yourself.”
> — *Rachel Stone*
